Half-Remembered

Twelve empty places that once held people.
Nothing here can hurt you. Nothing here is quite right.

  • One controller. Only your right hand is used. Leave the left one on the table.
  • Comfort first. Snap turning and a movement vignette are on by default. Seated or standing, blink-teleport, speed and more live in the Journal (press B).
  • No danger. No violence, no jump scares, no fail state. Find three memories in each place, then open the door.
  • Bright flash warning. The finale has a slow flash of white light. It can be switched off in the Journal.
  • About 60–90 minutes. Progress saves automatically on this device.
